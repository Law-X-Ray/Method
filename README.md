# Law-X-Ray

### Methodology for the Reconstruction of Institutional Vulnerabilities in Legislation

Law-X-Ray is an analytical methodology for the step-by-step deductive reconstruction of institutional mechanisms of corruption vulnerabilities and risks arising from the architecture of normative acts.

The methodology examines how the interaction of legal provisions, institutional arrangements, and oversight structures may create conditions for corrupt influence, concealment of information, distortion of decisions, or circumvention of oversight.

## Methodological Approach

The reconstruction of institutional mechanisms is based on a defined set of grounds:

- Normative facts
- Logical inferences
- Generally recognized theories

Independent empirical evidence, where available and applicable, serves as an additional means of corroborating the reconstructed mechanisms and substantive conclusions.

The reconstruction proceeds step by step from the applicable grounds to conclusions, allowing each substantive conclusion to be independently examined and verified.

## Analytical Structure

Law-X-Ray distinguishes three levels in the reconstruction of institutional mechanisms:

- **Mechanism** — a reconstructed causal structure describing how an institutional vulnerability may operate.
- **Channels of Realization** — specific ways in which the mechanism may be carried out.
- **Operational Details** — more specific practical or technical aspects of a channel, where applicable.

These levels form a nested analytical structure in which each mechanism may contain one or more channels of realization, and each channel may include operational details.

## Scope and Limitations

Law-X-Ray does not establish the occurrence of corruption or other legal violations, determine legal liability, or constitute a comprehensive assessment of a normative act.

The methodology is designed to reconstruct and examine potential institutional vulnerabilities arising from regulatory architecture. It does not guarantee the identification of all possible mechanisms, vulnerabilities, or risks.

## Project Structure

- **Method** — description and development of the Law-X-Ray methodology.
- **Analyses** — analytical applications of the methodology to legislation across different jurisdictions.

Law-X-Ray is an independently developed research and analytical project.
